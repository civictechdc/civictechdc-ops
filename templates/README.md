# Templates

Reusable structures, not completed private records, approved commitments, or
instructions to take an external action. New templates below are drafts,
reviewed November 3, 2026; maintaining role is unassigned.

- [Project brief](project-brief.md) — need, users, scope, capacity, and continuity.
- [Task brief](task-brief.md) — a bounded request for a human or assistant.
- [Decision record](decision-record.md) — what was decided, why, and by whom
  under what authority.
- [Event drafts](../integrations/luma/event-templates/README.md) — five
  placeholder-only drafts, kept with their related integration.
- [Media-request record](../knowledge/media-request-workflow.md#minimal-private-record)
  — part of the unapproved media workflow; completed records belong in a
  restricted location, never this public repository.

Use the [resource guide](../docs/RESOURCE-GUIDE.md) for provenance, review,
approval, and privacy expectations. Filling in a field does not create
authority, participation, or consent.
