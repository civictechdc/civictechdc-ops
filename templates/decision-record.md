# Decision record — draft template

**Status:** draft template, not a decision. **Reviewed:** November 3, 2026.
**Maintaining role:** unassigned. **Provenance:** starter structure for
preserving actual, appropriately shareable decisions and their rationale.

```text
Decision title / record date:
State: proposed / decided / superseded
Question and context:
Relevant sources / check dates:
Options considered:
Decision, if actually made:
Who had authority / actual approval record and date:
Rationale / tradeoffs:
Consequences / follow-up work:
Uncertainty / conditions for revisiting:
Agreed maintaining role, if any:
Superseded by / related records:
```

Do not invent an approver, turn a discussion into a decision, or publish private
deliberations or approval-record links. A public summary must be suitable for
public sharing and accurately distinguish a proposal from an adopted decision.
