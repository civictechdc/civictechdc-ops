# Media-request workflow

**Unapproved discussion draft.** Prepared November 3, 2026.
This document designates no spokesperson and grants no permission to contact
anyone, make a public statement, or connect a service.

## Proposed workflow

1. **Capture minimally.** Record receipt date, outlet, requester/public
   professional contact, topic, format, deadline with timezone, and stated
   interview/recording terms. Avoid unrelated personal or sensitive information.
2. **Route privately.** Confirm a restricted coordination route and responsible
   person. Do not assume previous contacts, permissions, or response targets
   remain current. Acknowledgments require authorization and should not promise
   an interview.
3. **Clarify and verify.** Independently confirm the requester and outlet.
   Ask about intended use, scope, format, timing, and any payment or sponsorship
   arrangement. Assess concrete accuracy/privacy risks; do not assume quote
   review or editorial approval will be available.
4. **Decide explicitly.** An authorized human decides whether to respond,
   clarify, defer, or decline, and selects a spokesperson and scope. Escalate
   sensitive personnel, financial, safety, privacy, and partner matters.
5. **Prepare a short brief.** Separate verified facts and sources from open
   questions. Confirm project status, attributed quotations, partner consent,
   and any statistical or legal-status claims. Do not promise future work or
   events.
6. **Review before sending.** Obtain approval of the exact written response or
   interview brief. Confirm who may speak or send it, and clarify recording and
   attribution terms. Do not assume an off-the-record agreement exists.
7. **Close the loop privately.** Record the decision, approver, approved
   version, sender, date, and outcome in an authorized restricted location.
   Keep only necessary personal data. Corrections or follow-ups need review.

## Minimal private record

```text
Received / deadline / timezone:
Outlet / requester / verified public contact:
Topic / format / intended use:
Responsible person / spokesperson / approver:
Decision / reason:
Facts checked / sources / check date:
Approved version / approval date:
Sent by / sent date / outcome / public coverage:
```

Do not store completed records or correspondence in this public repository.

## Before adoption

An authorized human must approve the workflow and choose the routing
destination, response target, spokesperson/backup, access and retention rules,
and review cadence. No action is authorized merely by completing this template.
