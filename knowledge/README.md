# Institutional knowledge

Save public-safe organizational context, verified lessons, and decision
rationale here so support work does not depend on one person's or assistant's
memory.

## Current material

- [Working principles](organizational-context.md) — discussion draft covering
  user involvement, realistic capacity, and continuity.
- [Media-request workflow](media-request-workflow.md) — an unapproved process
  draft, also indexed under [workflows](../workflows/README.md).
- [Decision-record template](../templates/decision-record.md) — a starting
  point for preserving actual decisions; no completed organizational decision
  is implied by the template.

## What to retain

Useful public-safe examples include a confirmed process, a lesson from a
completed activity, or a decision with its rationale and actual approval.
Include sources, review date, uncertainty, and the responsible maintaining
role if one has agreed.

Do not save raw private conversations, personal profiles, inferred consent,
outdated rosters, or assistant speculation as institutional facts. Link to
maintained sources instead of creating a competing copy.
